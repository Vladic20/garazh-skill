# duet — "Claude Duet builds the studio" (130 s)

**One sentence:** one agent working alone is slow and sequential; several agents working at once split the work, settle their
own conflicts and converge on a finished product — the repo and the working studio of tvhead-studio.
**Viewer:** X / YouTube, sound optional. **Format:** 1920×1080, 60 fps, H.264, AAC. **Text:** none until the final card
("Claude Duet", small "tvhead-studio"); file names on the blocks are part of the world.

## Style card
Dark glass studio (#0b0c10) with a cyan wire scaffold and a grid floor; four glass platforms with coloured edges; clay agents
(coordinator orange #e5744f and 1.35× larger; workers teal, lilac, yellow, blue); pieces in each zone's colour. Warm key from
the front-left, cool rim, soft shadows, accumulation motion blur and depth of field. Music 120 BPM: ambient alone, synthwave
together; landings on the beat grid.

## Direction notes (what changed from the first idea)
- **One persistent actor:** the coordinator is the lone builder at the start and the one who presses "done" at the end.
- **The cut lands on an action:** at 16 s he is still carrying the third piece; he stomps, and the loose pieces sort themselves
  into four piles in flight — the task split happens on screen, not in a caption.
- **Cause → effect everywhere:** every grab, throw, insert, squeeze and check visibly changes the build (a slot fills, a cable
  lights, the preview starts playing real frames from tvhead-studio renders).
- **Silence as an accent:** the music drops out for a bar and a half on the tear, then comes back as one worker yields.
- **Hierarchy over chaos:** in the parallel block the camera alternates 2-second close-ups with wide "everyone at once" shots,
  so the eye always knows where to look.
