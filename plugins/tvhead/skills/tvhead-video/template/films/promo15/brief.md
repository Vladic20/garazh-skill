# promo15 — 15-second hardcore kinetic promo for the repo (X / Twitter)

**One sentence:** one prompt becomes a whole video — tvhead-studio is a free, open-source Claude Code skill for it.
**Viewer:** devs and creators scrolling X with the sound off. **Length:** 15 s. **Format:** 1920×1080, 60 fps, H.264, AAC.
**Facts allowed (from the repo):** Claude Code skill · MIT · 13 looks · EN/RU · free voices by default · 16:9 and 9:16 ·
generated music · one-click app · 3D films · github.com/Vladic20/tvhead-studio. Nothing else is claimed.

## Style card — "hype"
Near-black `#0b0b0c`, bone white `#f4f1ea`, one hot accent `#ff5b1f` (the clay cube's family); full-frame accent cards with black type.
Heavy grotesque caps (Rubik 800) extruded in 3D by stacked layers; JetBrains Mono micro HUD labels (timecode, counters, crosshair).
Motion: hits on beats, eighth-note cuts only where the anchor stays put, accumulation motion blur on every fast move, shake decays
in 0.3 s. Music: `hype` preset, 150 BPM (beat 0.4 s, bar 1.6 s), grid from 0.12 s; drums drop out for the end card.

## Thread
A typed prompt dives into the screen, turns into giant words, explodes into 13 looks flying past, gains a voice, music and both
formats, races through the pipeline with the clay cube riding it, and lands as the wordmark with the URL.

## Variants (heavy scenes)
- **Opening** — A: a terminal types the prompt, the camera dives through the cursor ✔ (shows the product action in second 1);
  B: a 3-2-1 counter slam; C: "STOP EDITING." glitch title. A wins: the hook *is* the product.
- **Key scene** — A: 3D corridor of real look frames flying past on eighths with a counter ✔; B: a 13-tile grid flipping;
  C: one frame morphing through looks. A wins: depth, motion blur, and the count lands on the grid at the end anyway.
- **Finale** — A: tiles collapse into the extruded wordmark slam + URL typing ✔; B: a deck of frames becomes the logo;
  C: the cube drops the logo. A wins: fastest to read, best poster frame.
